Do not try it anyway. A generator that cannot supply the starting surge leaves the motor stalled and drawing locked-rotor current. That is how a cheap outage becomes a dead central ac, 3 ton.
Never backfeed a generator through a dryer or range outlet. It energises the utility line outside your house and it kills the linemen working to restore your power. It is also illegal nearly everywhere. Use a transfer switch or an interlock kit, installed by an electrician. That is a few hundred dollars against somebody's life.
